What's the reasoning behind the quiz? +

Our original questions are informed by Holland's RIASEC theory of work interests and linked to responsibilities from the Scoot role descriptions supplied to the project team. We select three questions per career to keep the quiz balanced.

Responses indicate interest in the sampled activities; they do not measure ability, predict job performance or provide validated suitability percentages. Role-to-interest mappings and questions still require review by Scoot employees.

See all six interest scores and how we calculated them +

Each career's interest score = average of three responses (1–5). Similar scores are normal because jobs share work activities. Scores aren't likelihoods or job-suitability percentages.

Questions draw from supplied Scoot role descriptions and a RIASEC-informed approach; they are not an official O*NET test and have not been validated by Scoot.
